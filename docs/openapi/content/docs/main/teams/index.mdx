<Cards>
  <Card title="Create a team" href="/teams/post-teams-new">
    An admin can assign any active manager or admin as manager, a manager always becomes the manager of the team. The end of the working day must be after its start.
  </Card>
  <Card title="List teams" href="/teams/post-teams-list">
    Cursor paginated list ordered by creation time. Admins see every team, managers see the teams they manage or belong to, employees see the teams they belong to.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Update teams in bulk" href="/teams/patch-teams">
    Applies the same data to every id. Authorization is checked for every item before any write. Managers can only update teams they manage and cannot change the manager.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Delete teams in bulk" href="/teams/delete-teams">
    Permanently deletes teams and their memberships. Admin only. Authorization is checked for every item before any deletion.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Retrieve a team" href="/teams/get-teams-by-id">
    Admins can retrieve any team, managers the teams they manage or belong to, employees the teams they belong to.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Archive a team" href="/teams/post-teams-by-id-archive">
    Archive a team. Admins can archive any team, managers the teams they manage.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Restore a team" href="/teams/post-teams-by-id-restore">
    Restore an archived team. Admins can restore any team, managers the teams they manage.
  </Card>
</Cards>
