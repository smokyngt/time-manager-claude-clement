---
title: Roles
description: The three roles of Time Manager, what each can do, and what a managed user is.
---

Every user has exactly one role: `employee`, `manager` or `admin`. The role decides which [scopes](../../api/scopes) your access token holds and which records you can see.

| Role | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| `employee` | Clock in and out, edit their own profile, see their own clocks and reports, and see the teams they belong to. |
| `manager` | Everything an employee can do, and create employees, create and run teams they manage, manage the members of those teams, and see or correct clocks and reports of those members. |
| `admin` | Everything, on every user, team and clock. |

## Managed users

A managed user, relative to a manager, is a user who belongs to at least one non-archived team that the manager manages. Archiving a team ends the management relationship through that team. If the user is in no other team of that manager, the manager no longer sees their clocks or reports.

Admins manage everyone. Employees manage nobody.

For user administration only, a manager can also act on an unassigned employee: an employee who belongs to no non-archived team. This lets a manager create an employee and then place them in a team. Clocks, reports and team data never use this extension.

## Visibility

Records outside your visibility behave as if they do not exist. Asking for a user, team or clock you cannot see returns `404`, not `403`. A `403` means the record is visible to you but the action is not allowed, for example an employee trying to change their own `role`.

## Role changes

Scopes are derived from the role when the access token is issued. A role change applies to new access tokens, so it takes effect at the next refresh, within the access token lifetime (15 minutes by default).

Only admins can set or change a role, and only admins can create managers or admins.
